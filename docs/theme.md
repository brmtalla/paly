High-design, editorial-grade interface with a calm, academic-luxury feel. The UI is minimal, intentional, and quietly confident — designed to feel like a personal study sanctuary rather than a productivity tool. The foundation is built on soft off-whites, warm paper tones, and deep charcoal neutrals, allowing content to breathe and remain the focal point.

Each user is assigned a single personalized accent color at onboarding. This color becomes their identity within the app — subtle, consistent, and never overwhelming. It appears in controlled moments only: section dividers, progress indicators, selection states, subtle gradients, and micro-highlights. The rest of the interface remains neutral, ensuring the personal color feels special and owned, not decorative.

Glassmorphism is present but restrained. Cards and panels feature light frosted translucency with crisp edges and generous spacing. Background blur is soft and purposeful, maintaining legibility and focus. The effect is tactile and modern, like studying at a clean desk under diffused light.

Typography is editorial and intentional. Large, confident headlines establish hierarchy, paired with refined, highly readable body text. Text colors are primarily soft black, charcoal, and warm gray. The user’s accent color is used sparingly for emphasis, reinforcing memory and identity without distraction.

Motion is smooth, deliberate, and calming. Transitions glide gently, never playful or bouncy. Animations feel supportive and reassuring, encouraging focus rather than stimulation. State changes are communicated through subtle fades, shifts, and controlled color reveals.

The overall mood is focused, personal, and premium. Paly feels like a quiet, beautifully designed space that belongs to the user alone — calm, intelligent, and steady. The interface never competes for attention. It exists to support memory, clarity, and consistency.
