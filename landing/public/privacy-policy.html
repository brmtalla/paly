Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Paly ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Paly mobile application. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our app.

Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: Email address and password (managed by Supabase Auth). Optionally, your full name.
  • Class & Study Data: Class schedules, notes you create, uploaded documents (PDFs, DOCX, PPTX, text files), AI-synthesized study content, quiz attempts, and study prompts.
  • Phone Number: Only recorded if you text us first. To receive Paly Pro study texts you send us a message containing your account's link code; we store the number that message came from so we can deliver to it via SendBlue. You can stop at any time by replying STOP, and we never obtain your number any other way.
  • Device Information: Push notification tokens for delivering in-app notifications. We collect your device platform (iOS/Android) for notification delivery.
  • Usage Data: Reading streaks, Paly Points, prompt read status, and quiz completion data to personalize your study experience.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the Paly study companion service
  • To generate AI-powered study materials from your notes and uploads using OpenAI
  • To schedule and deliver study prompts via push notifications and/or SMS
  • To track your study progress, streaks, and quiz performance
  • To send you service-related communications

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Supabase: Database hosting and authentication
  • OpenAI: AI-powered content synthesis (your notes and uploads are sent to OpenAI's API for processing)
  • SendBlue: SMS/iMessage delivery for study reminders (only if you opt in)
  • RevenueCat: Subscription and purchase management for Paly Pro
  • Expo: Push notification delivery

Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely in Supabase (hosted on AWS). We use row-level security policies to ensure users can only access their own data. Authentication tokens are stored securely on your device.

AI-Generated Content

When you upload documents or create notes, this content may be sent to OpenAI's API to generate study materials (summaries, flashcards, quiz questions, and daily study chunks). OpenAI processes this data according to their API data usage policies.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data through the app
  • Update or correct your information via Settings > Account
  • Delete your account and all associated data via Settings > Account > Delete Account
  • Opt out of SMS notifications at any time via Settings > Notifications

Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, all associated data is permanently removed from our systems.

Children's Privacy

Paly is designed for students aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected data from a child under 13, please contact us immediately.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy in the app and updating the "Last updated" date.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at support@paly.app.
